Exhibit 02

Reimagining how organizations learn from users.

Design With Us

Like many organizations, VMware’s conference research generated participation, but relatively little organizational learning or strategic alignment. Individual studies answered immediate questions, yet rarely created a repeatable way for Product, Design, Engineering, and Research to identify emerging priorities and think ahead together over time.

Design With Us redesigned the research ecosystem itself. Participatory design, iterative experimentation, cross-functional collaboration, and rigorous evaluation transformed conference research into a continuously improving learning system that connected user insight to product strategy long after each event ended.

Learning before products are finished.

Early Access & Beta

Many beta programs generate feedback. Far fewer generate evidence that is structured, synthesized, and delivered early enough to influence product decisions.

This mixed-method learning system combined digital feedback, interviews, behavioral signals, and structured evaluation to transform hundreds of user observations into prioritized evidence before general availability.

136 users • 547 feedback submissions • 23+ follow-up interviews

Designing continuous organizational learning.

Unified Feedback & Telemetry

Organizations rarely lack user feedback. They lack the ability to learn from it consistently.

Feedback often exists in disconnected systems, inconsistent formats, and incompatible measures, making it difficult to compare findings or build knowledge over time.

Standardized feedback, telemetry, governance, and question design improve evidence quality while making organizational learning continuous rather than episodic.

Building confidence in evidence.

Building Evidence Literacy

Trust begins long before a decision is made.

Evidence is useful when people understand where it comes from, what it represents, and where its limitations lie. Making evaluation methods visible helps teams build confidence in the evidence they use and the decisions they make.
